Private member apps for community associations

Pottalink gives a community association one private, members-only place for its member list, announcements, documents, events and governance records.

How it works

Each community gets its own space in the Pottalink app. Only people the community has approved can see that space. Officers manage members, documents and events; members read announcements, find documents and take part in events.

Members sign in with their mobile phone number. Pottalink sends a one-time sign-in code by text message, and members can then add a passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) so later sign-ins skip the text.

Our first community

Pottalink's first community is the Houston Haalipular Association (HHA), a community association in Houston, Texas. Membership is decided by HHA; to join, contact the association.

Text messages

Pottalink sends sign-in codes and a small number of account messages by SMS. See the SMS terms for how to opt in and out, and the privacy policy for how we handle your information.
