Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026.

This policy explains how Pottalink, operated by Chernor Jalloh in Houston, Texas (“Pottalink”, “we”), handles information about members of the communities that use the Pottalink app and admin website.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your mobile phone number and name.
  • Membership information: the communities you belong to, your member ID, your roles and groups, and your membership status.
  • What you add: messages, posts, documents and questions you send in the app, and your responses to events and votes.
  • Sign-in and security information: sign-in times, the public part of any passkey you add, and security logs. We never receive your fingerprint or face data; it stays on your device.
  • Device information: a push notification token for your device, and technical error reports when the app crashes.
  • Text message consent: whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your community's content only to its approved members, according to the roles your community sets.
  • To send sign-in codes, account messages and the notifications you have turned on.
  • To answer questions in the app's assistant from your community's own documents.
  • To fix problems and protect the service from abuse.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Other approved members and officers of your community see the information your community's settings allow, for example your name in the member list. Your phone number is shown only to roles your community allows, and each time someone views it, it is recorded.

We use service providers to run Pottalink, and they process information only on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, database, file storage, text messages, email and the assistant's AI model.
  • Expo: delivering push notifications to your device.
  • Sentry: technical error reports from the apps.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Sharing with service providers that help us run the service, such as the text message provider, is permitted. Text message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every request is checked on our servers for a valid sign-in, an active membership and the right role, and the database itself limits each request to one community.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a member of a community on Pottalink. You can delete your account in the app; we then delete your personal information, except records we must keep by law or that your community needs for its official records, which keep only your member ID.

Your choices

  • Turn notification categories on or off in the app.
  • Reply STOP to stop text messages (see the SMS terms).
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@pottalink.com.

Children

Pottalink is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell members in the app before important changes take effect.
