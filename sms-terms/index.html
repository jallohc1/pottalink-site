SMS terms

Program: Pottalink Sign-In Codes. Effective November 17, 2026.

What you receive

Pottalink sends text messages to members of communities that use the Pottalink app:

  • One-time sign-in codes, sent only when you ask for one on the sign-in screen.
  • Account messages, such as the message telling you that your community approved your membership and giving your member ID.

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

How you opt in

When you sign up in the Pottalink app, you enter your own mobile number and check this box, which is not checked for you:

“I agree to receive sign-in codes and account messages from Pottalink by text message. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See the SMS terms and privacy policy.”

We record the date and time you agreed. Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You receive one code each time you ask to sign in, plus occasional account messages. Most members receive only a few messages a month.

Cost

Pottalink does not charge for text messages. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any Pottalink message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one message confirming you have opted out. To start again, reply START. If you opt out, you can still sign in with a passkey you added earlier, but you cannot receive sign-in codes by text.

Help

Reply HELP to any Pottalink message, or email support@pottalink.com.

Coverage

Text messages are sent to mobile numbers in the United States. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

We use your mobile number only to run the service. Mobile numbers and text message consent are never sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing. See the privacy policy.
